# The Ember Crown: Encounters & XP (v0.1)

*Companion to Story_Bible.md and DECISIONS.md. All XP values are **per character** (party of 4) unless marked.*
*Math uses the 2014 DMG encounter rules and the PHB leveling table (verified from the user's PDFs). Monster stats are from SRD 5.1 / the Monster Manual. CR/XP values are from memory: verify each in the MM (see CONFIRM LOCALLY).*

---

## 1. Design goals (from the user)
- **XP for everything:** fights, quest stages, discoveries and smart talk. A fight avoided by talking, sneaking or bribing gives **the same XP** as winning it.
- **Main path only:** the party ends Act 1 at **level 3**, Act 2 at **level 4**, and faces the finale at **level 5**.
- **About 80% of optional content done:** the party hits **level 6 (the cap) during Act 3, before the finale**, about +1 level ahead of the main path the whole way. (In this design, about 62% completion is the minimum that still reaches level 6.)
- **Each act:** 7 mandatory fights (inside the DMG's 6–8-per-adventuring-day guideline), up to 4 optional fights, up to 4 side quests with 2–3 parts each, and random travel encounters (§5b).
- **Telegraphing:** the party should always know when it is about to walk into a fight (§6).

## 3. Encounter math reference (DMG, verified)
- **Party-of-4 difficulty thresholds:**
  - L1: 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 (Easy / Medium / Hard / Deadly)
  - L2: 200 / 400 / 600 / 800
  - L3: 300 / 600 / 900 / 1,600
  - L4: 500 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000
  - L5: 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 / 4,400
  - L6: 1,200 / 2,400 / 3,600 / 5,600
- **Encounter multipliers** (count of monsters): 1 = ×1, 2 = ×1.5, 3–6 = ×2, 7–10 = ×2.5.
- **XP actually awarded = the monsters' raw XP ÷ 4**, with no multiplier (the multiplier is only for judging difficulty).
- **Why quests still carry most of the XP:** even 7 fights per act can't level a party by themselves, because 5e expects about one level per 6–8 fights at low levels and more later. So quest stages supply about 60% of main-path XP, and combat XP keeps fights rewarding.
- **Difficulty setting does not change XP.** Easy, Medium and Hard add or remove enemies, but the XP award for each encounter is fixed, so the level curve stays stable on every setting.

## 5b. Random travel encounters

- **When:** only on travel legs *between* areas (Ashford ↔ Old Mine road, Ashford ↔ the Marsh of Chelimber, marsh ↔ abbey, Ashford ↔ the high Greypeaks), plus once per long rest taken in the wild. **Never** inside towns, dungeons, or when moving between streets or rooms.
- **How (DMG default, verified):** roll a d20 once per leg; on **18 or higher** an encounter happens (a 15% chance), then roll a d8 on the region's table. The roll is shown in the log.
- **Still telegraphed:** a passive Perception check decides whether you spot them first and get the "Prepare" step, or they surprise you.
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Medium for the expected level, scaled by the Easy / Medium / Hard setting. Some are talk-or-fight.
- **XP:** normal monster XP, as a bonus outside the main ledger (expect 0–2 per act).

## 6. Fight telegraphing (no surprise fights the player didn't see coming)
1. **Dialogue that will start a fight is marked** with a crossed-swords icon and "(starts combat)". There's always a non-violent option unless the scene is an ambush.
2. **Hostile NPCs are visible before engaging:** a red outline and a hover tooltip ("Hostile · looks dangerous").
3. **Ambushes are foreshadowed by the world** ("the birdsong stops"). The party's passive Perception is checked: on success, a "You spot movement in the trees" prompt lets you prepare; on failure, the enemies get surprise (standard 5e).
4. **Pre-combat preparation:** before any fight the player can see coming, there's a short "Prepare" step to cast buffs and reposition, then Begin.
5. **The journal marks optional fights** with an estimated difficulty for your current party level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Deadly) using the DMG math above.
6. **Autosave before every fight.**

## 7. Wix (camp companion) (approved)
- **Role:** non-combat companion. He hides during fights, travels with the party, comments on places, and tells jokes at short and long rests (more at long rests).
- **Small mechanical perk:** once per long rest, "Wix's Distraction": one enemy has disadvantage on its next attack roll (he throws a pie) [inferred; tune later].
- **Personality:** cheerful coward, food-obsessed, makes up proverbs, terrified of "the Old Red", fiercely loyal once fed.
- **Sample lines (original, for tone):**
  - *"Goblin wisdom: never trust a bridge that's nicer than you."*
  - *"Why do humans put the pie on the windowsill? Is it a gift? It feels like a gift."*
  - *(entering the Kiln)* "Okay. Okay. If the mountain eats us, I want it known I was against this. Loudly. From the back."
  - *(at a long rest, after Act II)* "Brannoc says I'm 'part of the family' now. I asked which part. He said 'the part that eats everything.' Best day of my life."
